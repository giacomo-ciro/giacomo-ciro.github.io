# About me
I am pursuing a [MSc in Artificial Intelligence](https://www.unibocconi.it/en/programs/master-science/artificial-intelligence) at [Bocconi University](https://www.unibocconi.it/en) after having graduated cum laude with a [BSc in Economics and Computer Science](https://www.unibocconi.it/en/programs/bachelor-science/economics-management-and-computer-science).

I am currently working on 3D egocentric vision at the [Computer Vision and Geometry Group (CVG)](https://cvg.ethz.ch/), with [Dr. Zuria Bauer](https://zuriabauer.com/), [Prof. Marc Pollefeys](https://people.inf.ethz.ch/marc.pollefeys/) and [Prof. Chiara Plizzari](https://chiaraplizz.github.io/).

Previously, I worked on scaling laws for deep learning models in transcriptomics with [Prof. Francesca Buffa](https://cs.unibocconi.eu/people/francesca-buffa), and on making text diffusion models more efficient with [Prof. Dirk Hovy](https://www.dirkhovy.com/).

I co-founded [Bocconi Students for Machine Learning (BSML)](https://www.bsmachinelearning.com/) to connect minds, not only layers.
